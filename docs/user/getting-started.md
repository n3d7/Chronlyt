# Getting Started

After installation, choose **Sign in / Create account** or **Continue without account**, then choose a theme and weekly focus target, decide whether to enable local Activity Check-ins, optionally add one Goal, choose whether Chronlyt may stay ready in the background or start at login, and accept the starter Dashboard. Accounts and autostart remain optional. Every choice can be changed later.

1. Open **Focus**, choose a duration, describe one concrete outcome and start your first Focus Session.
2. Open **Tasks**, add a task, then move it to Planned or In Progress.
3. Use **Brain Dump** for an idea you do not want to classify yet; convert it to a Task later.
4. An **Activity Check-in** is a short local prompt asking what is happening now. Disable or change its interval in Settings.
5. Open **Statistics** to choose a period and arrange the factual metrics you care about.
6. Open **Weekly Review** to compare facts with your reflection.
7. Open **Export & Backup → Data Export** for offline Markdown/JSON.
8. If desired, open **Settings → AI** to configure a provider. Nothing is sent until preview and explicit consent.

An account is only an identity foundation for future features. It does not upload your existing local history. See [Accounts](accounts.md).

If Background Mode is enabled, closing the window hides Chronlyt in the tray. Use **Quit Chronlyt** in the tray to exit completely. See [Background Mode and Start at Login](background-and-startup.md).
